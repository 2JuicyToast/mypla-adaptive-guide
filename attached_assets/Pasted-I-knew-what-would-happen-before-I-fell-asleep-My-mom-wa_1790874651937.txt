I knew what would happen before I fell asleep. My mom wanted somebody to go with her. I said no. I did not want to sit in the car and watch her leave Max at that dog place. I knew I would probably feel sick or cry. I also did not want to be around my mom. So I went to sleep and hoped I would miss the whole thing. At the time, that did feel easier than saying goodbye.

My mom made that decision for a reason. We had three dogs, and they were a lot to handle. Max was our first dog. Then we got Coco, and they had puppies, and we kept Chloe. She was the runt of the litter and the only girl. Max was the hardest to care for. He was loud and had a lot of energy. He could be aggressive toward people he did not know. He also kept peeing and pooping on the couches. I knew that was a problem, and it made sense why my mom felt we could not keep all three dogs. Regardless, I was still not okay with Max being the one to go.

My brother and I did not even know for sure if Max was gone forever. My mom said we might take him back if nobody chose him after two weeks. I kept thinking about that. I really wanted to believe he would come back home, and I would not have to accept that he was really gone for good. I did not want to say goodbye because it felt like saying he was gone for good.

When I woke up, my dog Max was gone. The house looked the same. Our other dogs, Coco and Chloe, were still there. The couches Max always had accidents on were still there too. My mom came home, but Max was not with her. I had gone to sleep because I did not want to see her take him away. Now I was awake, and I had to deal with it anyway.

My brother and I did not get to make the decision. My mom was the one taking care of the house and its problems. She decided Max had to go. I was mad because I could not do anything to change her mind. I did not want to get rid of him, so I became blunt and mean instead. My mom tried to talk to me about Max, but I gave her short answers, and sometimes I would just say “okay” or “fine” to end the conversation. I probably looked like I did not care, but I actually cared a lot. I just could not talk about it without feeling like I might be sick or crying.

Then I started crying. I was yelling at the same time. It was not like when I joke around and pretend to be upset. I could not stop it or hide it. It was one of the hardest times I have ever cried. I was sad about Max. I was angry that I had no say. I felt guilty that my mom had to do it by herself. I was also scared we would not get him back.

That is something I do a lot. I can pretend to cry jokingly or act dramatic when nothing serious is happening, but when I am really sad, it is really hard for me to show it. I start to get quiet or irritated even though I want to explain how I feel, but the words never come out right.

When my mom came home that day without Max, she was hurt that nobody had gone with her. She was frustrated because we acted like she wanted to give him away. Then she came home to me barely talking to her and being short. I gave her the silent treatment even though she was upset too.

When she left, she was sad, and she wanted someone with her. Taking Max away was really hard for her. Even though I knew that, I was just stuck thinking about how unfair it felt to me. I did not go with her, and I did not help her through it. Staying home did not let us keep Max; it just meant she had to do that part alone.

For a while, I kept blaming her. It was easier to be mad at my mom than to think about Max being somewhere he did not know. I wanted to avoid thinking about how confused he might feel or that I had not said goodbye. I knew my mom made the choice she thought was best for our family. I just did not want to admit it then.

It is hard to remember everything she said that day. I remember going to my room after hearing how hurt she was. I could not keep acting like I was fine. Max was gone, and I had slept through him leaving. My mom had gone without us, and all the feelings I had been avoiding caught up with me at once.

I still miss Max. I can admit now that three dogs were a lot for us. His behavior was hard to manage too. Another home might be better for him, I guess, and I understand more about why my mom made that decision, and I can understand all of that and still feel hurt when I think about him.

I wish I had been honest with my mom about why I did not want to go. I was scared of seeing Max leave. I also found it hard to explain that I understood but still hated the decision at the same time. Maybe she would have understood me better if I had said that out loud. Even talking about Max now can make my eyes hurt and my head feel hot, like I might cry. I thought sleeping through the trip would save me from the worst part, but it did not. I just had to feel it worse later. I learned that acting like I do not care will not make the feeling go away. It only makes it harder to tell people around me what is really wrong.
